Official release promoted from the nightly build that was tested on the K1C (same binary). It summarizes the `v0.35.x` nightlies (`v0.35.1` to `v0.35.8`).

## Highlights

- **The CFS on the screen**: the Home screen shows the CFS and a new four-slot CFS screen lists the spools of the box. Tap a spool to edit its material and color from the screen; the color is sent in the format the box accepts, with a retry in another format if it refuses the first.
- **Camera on and off from the title bar**: the camera icon at the top right turns the camera service off and on with one tap (crossed out and gray when off, green when on). It asks for confirmation when you turn it off during a print, because the timelapse takes its photos with the camera, and it tells you when the Power Macros that do it are not installed. It needs the Power Script Power Macros (`CAMERA_OFF` and `CAMERA_ON`).
- **Power Update**: the PowerScreen button checks for updates the same way the Power Script one does (*Checking...*, *UPDATE* or *Check for updates*) instead of staying on *Update*.
- **Print Status**: a taller thumbnail, and the file name, state, percentage and progress bar now sit right above the Pause and Cancel buttons, with no empty gap.
- **Cancel print** uses the PowerUI confirmation card, aligned with the rest of the theme, with *Cancel print* and *Keep printing*.

## Fixes

- Stray characters in the CFS spool editor.
- The color of a CFS spool was rejected by the box when it was sent with the leading hash.

## Documentation

- The PowerUI license and credits: intended for Creality K1 printers with root, free for people, commercial use by companies needs the author's permission; credit to shadcn/ui for the UI guidelines behind PowerUI.

## Known issue

- The CFS box can still show as disconnected now and then because the USB adapter of the CFS drops out of the printer's USB hub. This is a fault of the printer's hardware or firmware, not of PowerScreen. The Power Script has a diagnostics module that logs it and recovers the USB adapter.
